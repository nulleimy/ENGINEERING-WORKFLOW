# Code of Conduct

Work is evaluated through evidence, technical reasoning, product value and risk—not status, ego or authorship.

Participants must:

- communicate directly and respectfully;
- challenge ideas without attacking people;
- disclose uncertainty and conflicts of interest;
- preserve authorship and provenance;
- avoid unilateral destructive changes;
- resolve disagreement through recorded options, evidence and decision authority;
- protect private, personal, security-sensitive and proprietary information.

Harassment, intimidation, deliberate misrepresentation, sabotage, credential misuse and unauthorized reuse of another project’s code or assets are unacceptable.
