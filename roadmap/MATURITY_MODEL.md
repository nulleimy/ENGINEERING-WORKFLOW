# Engineering Maturity Model

## Level 0 — Uncontrolled

Unknown baseline, undocumented ownership, manual release and unverified recovery.

## Level 1 — Identified

Canonical project, owner, baseline, basic tests, status and manual evidence.

## Level 2 — Repeatable

Risk lanes, work packages, consistent commands, independent review for significant changes and documented release/recovery.

## Level 3 — Automated

Golden paths, CI quality gates, generated evidence, dependency/security scanning and environment automation.

## Level 4 — Trusted

Provenance-bearing artifacts, policy-as-code, progressive delivery, tested recovery, SLO/error-budget operation and strong source/build controls.

## Level 5 — Adaptive

AI-assisted controlled execution, automated context/evidence, measured flow, constraint-driven continuous improvement and portfolio-wide learning.

Projects target the level justified by risk and value; the goal is not maximum ceremony.
