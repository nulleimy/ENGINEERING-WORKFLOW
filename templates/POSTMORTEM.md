# Template — Blameless Postmortem

## Executive summary

Impact, duration, scope and recovery.

## What happened

Evidence-based timeline and system behavior.

## Contributing conditions

Technical, process, documentation, monitoring, dependency and decision conditions.

## What worked / failed

Detection, containment, recovery, communication and controls.

## Corrective actions

Each action has owner, priority, expected outcome and verification.

## Lessons

Reusable findings and control changes. Avoid vague actions such as “be more careful.”
